Earning the next generation

A challenger replaces the champion only after beating its score by more than 2.5 points on the same fresh self-play matches. This is a selection gate, not a statistical guarantee.

What counts as learning?

Competition is the only reward.

Fitness is the fraction of matches won, with half a point for exact ties. Territory, production, and movement are observed and visualized, but never added as training rewards.

The fixed panel contains the three best policies from generation zero. It uses 18 games on unseen seeds at sizes 16, 24, and 32. It measures progress beyond initialization, not strength against established bots.

Training is two-player self-play. The bot can enter larger games, but multiplayer strength needs separate evaluation.
